Privacy Policy — HopKids Publisher

Last updated: November 27, 2026

HopKids Publisher is an internal publishing tool used exclusively by the HopKids channel owner (@pip.hop_kids).

1. Data We Access

Using TikTok OAuth, the app requests only the permissions required to authenticate the HopKids account (user.info.basic) and upload/publish videos (video.upload, video.publish).

2. Data Storage & Sharing

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the channel owner's computer solely to publish scheduled videos. No personal data is collected from third-party users, sold, or shared with third parties.
